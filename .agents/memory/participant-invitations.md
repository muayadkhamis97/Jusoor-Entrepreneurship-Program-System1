---
name: Participant invitations
description: Invitation lifetime, delivery, and access boundaries.
---

Participant invitations expire 30 days after creation or renewal. Retrying the same pending invitation reuses its invitation record and does not duplicate an already delivered email; an expired one can be renewed by retrying the same email and business. Previously linked participants remain active after expiry.

**Why:** The user chose the recommended 30-day lifetime for participant invitations.

**How to apply:** Keep invitation email, schema defaults/backfill, and verified-email account linking consistent with this lifetime. Derive program scope from the selected existing business and do not link an expired invitation to an account until it is renewed.
