---
name: Jusoor grant ledger
description: Rules for recording and correcting received funds for demo-day awards.
---

Track each awarded demo-day decision in USD with individual, dated receipt entries. Keep entries append-only; correct a mistaken receipt by adding a reversal with a reason rather than deleting history. Only coordinators can manage the financial ledger. Do not initiate payments or store bank details as part of this workflow.

**Why:** The coordinator selected installment-based tracking. The coordinator is the platform administrator, and a durable correction trail is safer for grant records.

**How to apply:** Keep each ledger attached to its awarded pitch record; protect awards with receipt history from deletion, reassignment, or reducing the award below net receipts.
