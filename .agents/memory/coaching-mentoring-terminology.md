name: Coaching and mentoring terminology
description: The program's terms for coaching and mentoring
---

Coaching and mentoring refer to the same service, not separate program categories. Use “Mentor” and “Mentoring” consistently in user-facing interface copy.

**Why:** the user clarified that coaching and mentoring are the same and chose Mentor/Mentoring as the consistent interface wording.

**How to apply:** do not model them as distinct services or categories; use Mentor/Mentoring labels in navigation, forms, reports, and exports.
