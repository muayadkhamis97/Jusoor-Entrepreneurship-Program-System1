---
name: Guest judging forms
description: Why public judging forms use self-reported identity and a frozen rubric separate from signed-in judge scorecards.
---

Staff create shareable judging forms with required judge name/email, custom scoring criteria and written questions, covering several selected businesses.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested link access without sign-in and chose configurable questions and selected-business scope.

**How to apply:** Treat guest identity as self-reported, not verified. Keep guest assessments private to authorized program staff and separate from authenticated judge scorecards; do not silently include them in existing pitch scores.

Freeze the rubric and business selection after the first opening, including when closed and reopened.

**Why:** Changing a published scoring definition would make earlier and later assessments incomparable.

**How to apply:** A new rubric needs a new form, not edits that reinterpret existing responses.

Status-change audit history can identify the staff member and time only for changes recorded after tracking is introduced; earlier actor/timestamp data cannot be reconstructed.

**Why:** Existing forms retain only their current status, not a record of past status transitions.

**How to apply:** Do not invent or backfill historical actors or times. Make the tracking start limitation clear when reviewing history.
