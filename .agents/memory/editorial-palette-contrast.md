---
name: Editorial palette contrast
description: Why source palette fidelity does not imply safe small-text color pairings in the extracted design system.
---

Keep the extracted terracotta for large editorial emphasis, but measure small-text foreground/surface combinations when extending or applying Program Editorial. Do not restore source cream or muted colors indiscriminately to every semantic role in the name of fidelity.

**Why:** The source's warm off-whites on terracotta and its muted text on the darker paper surface fell below4.5:1 in actual contrast calculations. A very slight cream adjustment was still insufficient. Contrast-adapted semantic roles and small-text treatments were therefore intentional, not accidental deviations from the source.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction between raw source palette values and derived accessible role mappings when adding components or applying the system. Recheck the actual foreground/background pair, not merely whether each color appeared in the mockup.
